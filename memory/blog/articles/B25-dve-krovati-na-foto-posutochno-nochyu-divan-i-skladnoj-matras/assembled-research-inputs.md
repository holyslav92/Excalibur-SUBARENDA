## Guest mechanics / practical facts

1. «Спальное место» on platforms legally includes sofa-beds, folding cots, air mattresses — if disclosed in listing settings.
2. Problem is not that sofa can't sleep — problem is when photos/mental model say «two beds» but night reality is bed + worn sofa + floor mat.
3. Late check-in reduces options: rebooking same night in Tyumen costs more than savings from cheaper listing.
4. Three adults ≠ two double beds unless host prepared three real sleep surfaces with bedding.
5. «Основные» vs «дополнительные» спальные места — different comfort tier; folding mattress often in «дополнительные».
6. Studio/1-room economy segment skews to sofa (forum + host comments: space, cost).
7. Guest should ask before pay: count + TYPE per person + fresh photos of each surface + bedding for each.
8. Direct booking outside aggregator may lack structured sleeping-place fields — even more need for written confirmation.
9. B04 already covered paying for wrong guest COUNT at door — B25 is TYPE mismatch, not headcount surcharge.
