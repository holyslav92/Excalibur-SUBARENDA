## Core mechanics for Writer (from live sources)

### Standard time window
- Sutochno.ru (20.08.2025): industry standard check-in ~14:00, checkout ~12:00; hosts schedule cleaners at fixed times; late checkout often costs up to full day rate or requires approval.
- Rent365.net news (19.02.2026): standard 14:00 check-in / 12:00 checkout; 12:00–14:00 = cleaning, linen change, prep; late checkout until 17:00 = half day, after 17:00 = full day — only if calendar allows.
- Restfor.ru (updated 22.08.2026): from 01.03.2026 new RF hotel rules (PP №1912) — hotel sets check-in/out, gap ≤3h; late checkout fee per published tariff BEFORE booking; can refuse if next guest needs room.
- Bronevik.com (02.12.2025): typical hotel checkout 12:00; late checkout often 25–50% of day or half/full day depending on hour.

### What «late checkout fee» actually covers (host industry logic)
- RentTools.io (07.05.2026): fee is NOT for marginal hour of guest stay (utilities already paid) — it's for (1) cleaner overtime / lost cleaning slot, (2) risk next guest enters unfinished apartment. First hour after 11:00 cheap if next check-in after 16:00; if next guest same day before 14:00, late checkout should be refused regardless of price.
- RealtyCalendar blog (22.05.2026): «Уточните у гостей, во сколько они будут выезжать, — к этому времени нужно вызвать горничную»; cleaner checklist starts with «убедиться, что гости уехали».
- Sutochno.ru blog: if prepayment made, checkout time changes must be confirmed in booking system with guest consent.
