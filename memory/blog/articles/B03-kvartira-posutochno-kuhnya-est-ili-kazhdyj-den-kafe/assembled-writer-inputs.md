## research-notes.md (facts source — do not invent beyond this)

### reader_problem
Guest on 2–3 nights sees «кухня» in listing but doesn't know: stove+pan or kettle+microwave only. If kitchen is checkbox-only, guest doesn't treat it as café alternative.

### reader_outcome
Before paying, guest checks kitchen checklist: appliances, dishes, usage rules. Understands:
- when hotel with breakfast makes sense for 3 nights
- when apartment with working kitchen saves money
- questions to ask before booking
- why «кухня» alone guarantees nothing

### practical_facts
- «Кухня» means different equipment sets in different listings
- Minimum practical set: fridge, stove, microwave, kettle, dishes, sink; washing machine if claimed
- Microwave important for short trips — reheat ready food
- For stove cooking: verify pan and suitable dishes separately
- Dishes for 2–4 persons (dokrutim signal)
- Guests compare by specific items, not word «кухня»
- Pre-pay checklist: stove, pan, microwave, fridge, kettle, dishes, sink, can use all appliances, photos match description
- On arrival: check stove and fridge
- Short stay: verify «kettle + microwave» combo works
- Guests value basics: salt, oil, tea; without oil some order delivery
- Savings on café only with working kitchen + willingness to shop and wash dishes
- Hotel: standard breakfast, no cooking/cleaning
- 1–3 days: hotel may be better on fixed cost and convenience
- 3–5 days: hotel often still better; apartment rational if guest actually cooks
- 3 nights: choice depends on actual kitchen gear and meal plans, not label alone

### Добрый дом (tenant facts — no invented prices)
- Kitchen with dishes, appliances, linens, conditions stated upfront
- Contactless check-in, messenger reply within ~5 minutes
- Positioning: «как в отеле, но с домашними удобствами»
- Exact kitchen composition: verify on booking card or ask manager
- NO specific Добрый дом prices in text
