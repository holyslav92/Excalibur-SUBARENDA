## Normative / official (verified)

### PP RF 501 (16.04.2025) — in force from 01.09.2025
- p.23: employee must submit advance report within 3 business days after return; attach documents on hire of residential premises, travel, other trip expenses
- p.6: if no travel tickets, stay period confirmed by hire documents; in hotel — contract, cash receipt or BSO per PP 1853; if no hire/hotel docs — service note + confirmation from receiving party
- p.8–9: advance for travel, housing hire, per diem; reimbursement per employer local rules
URL: https://www.garant.ru/products/ipo/prime/doc/411790968/ (full text); also kontur.ru/articles/2322 (15 Aug 2025 overview)

- Apartment in business trip: expenses confirmed by hire contract + payment document (bank statement, online receipt)

## Practical facts for guest CASE

### Why «мы не гостиница» appears
- Private short-term host is not hotel under PP 1853; not obliged to issue hotel BSO/form 3-G «spravka» in hotel format
- BUT for business reimbursement apartment needs: договор найма/аренды + документ оплаты (чек, выписка, чек самозанятого)
- Employer/buhgalteria may habitually ask «spravka kak v gostinice» — mismatch of expectations, not automatic fraud

### What to ask BEFORE payment (lockpick)
1. Status: OOO, IP, or self-employed (NPD)?
2. Will you provide written hire contract with address, dates, amount, both signatures?
3. Payment confirmation: fiscal receipt / Moy nalog check / bank receipt with purpose «аренда жилья»?
4. Act of services or handover — if IP/OOO?
5. Can accounting get PDF samples of blank contract + receipt format before transfer?
6. If only «transfer to card» without check — high risk for advance report

### Self-employed landlord
- Contract alone NOT primary without Moy nalog payment check (business.go.yandex)
- Check must show landlord FIO, INN, date, purpose

### After trip
- Advance report + attachments within 3 business days (PP 501 p.23)
- Without supporting docs employer may pay employee from own funds but NOT deduct for profit tax (buhguru)
