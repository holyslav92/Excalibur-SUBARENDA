## Listing / amenity mechanics (practical)

- Short-term platforms and Avito cards often tick «стиральная машина» in amenities; photos may show washer in bathroom corner, but unit can be removed, broken, or belong to previous tenant/staging
- «Стиралка в подвале» / «на этаже общая» — frequent host reply when in-unit washer absent; guest who booked for in-apartment laundry still has mismatch if listing implied in-unit
- For 2–3 night stays, missing washer forces: hand-wash in sink, wear damp clothes on hanger, buy new socks/underwear, or spend evening at laundromat
- Washer is comfort amenity like kitchen/wifi — distinct from B23 checkout linen charge (post-stay billing) and B11 missing towels at arrival
- Before payment: ask «стиральная машина в этой квартире или общая?» + request 10–20 sec video pan of bathroom/utility corner showing washer outlet/hose
