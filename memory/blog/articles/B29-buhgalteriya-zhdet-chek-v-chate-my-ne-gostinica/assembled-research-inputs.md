- Official regulatory text mirrored: https://www.eg-online.ru/document/regulatory/303228/
- Substance: expenses for hiring dwelling for employees on business trip may be included in other expenses (subpara 12 para 1 art 264 NK RF); copy of landlord ownership documents NOT required; expenses must be justified and documented; documents may indirectly confirm stay (travel docs, lease contract, etc.)
